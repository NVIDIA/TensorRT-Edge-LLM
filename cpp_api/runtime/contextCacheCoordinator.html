Context Cache Coordinator#

class ContextCacheCoordinator#

Owns the complete host/device lifecycle around the CUDA-free ContextCacheManager.

The coordinator has no worker of its own; every call is made by the caller’s thread and callers must keep those calls serialized. Request handles may overlap in lifetime: several may be admitted and hold a page lease at once, which is the ownership shape in-flight batching needs.

Overlapping leases are not overlapping execution, and the distinction is load-bearing. Page-table rows bind to stable physical resident slots, while request-local reuse-length staging is reused across preparations. A prepare issued before the previous request’s async copy has landed would corrupt that staging buffer. A caller must therefore still finish one request’s steps before preparing the next; what a count rather than a flag permits is that the leases, not the execution, may interleave.

Normal publication occurs only at host-visible completion points already present in the runtime. An abnormal exit drains the bound stream before releasing active page references; a failed drain quarantines the entire request and poisons the coordinator until runtime-owned shutdown can establish quiescence. Poisoning gates new admissions, so a request already admitted when the stream failed is quarantined by its own drain rather than by the first failure.

Sequence-admission transaction

Every sequence joined through admitSequence is in admission until finalizeSequenceAdmission commits its final prefill chunk or abortSequenceAdmission discards it. Any number of sequences may be in admission at once, at any execution index, and each tracks its own in-flight device work. Per chunk the caller runs prepare -> (final chunk only: capture) -> its own stream synchronization -> complete -> advance (non-final) or finalize (final).

RequestHandle &request,
int32_t sequenceIndex
)#

Open the next prefill chunk of a sequence in admission. Before the first chunk this restores the sequence’s recurrent and partial-KV checkpoint into its stable resident slot without touching any other resident sequence. sequenceIndex is the transient execution index.

Reserve and enqueue the sequence’s prefill-end snapshot after its final chunk and before the runtime’s existing sampled-token synchronization. This is a no-op for non-hybrid deployments.

ContextCacheCoordinatorStatus completeSequenceAdmissionDeviceWork(
RequestHandle &request,
int32_t sequenceIndex
)#

Resolve the sequence’s open chunk after the runtime’s stream synchronization. Other sequences’ chunks stay open until their own completion.

ContextCacheCoordinatorStatus advanceSequenceAdmission(
RequestHandle &request,
int32_t sequenceIndex,
int32_t committedStateLength
)#

Record a committed non-final prefill chunk: the slot’s state now covers the first committedStateLength prompt tokens. Nothing is published and no snapshot is captured.

ContextCacheCoordinatorStatus abortSequenceAdmission(
RequestHandle &request,
int32_t sequenceIndex
)#

Discard a failed or cancelled sequence-admission transaction so ordinary eviction can retire it; the eviction’s compaction releases its lease. Drains the stream only when the sequence has an open chunk. The sequence never publishes afterwards. Throws for a sequence that is not in admission (finalized, aborted, or admitted with the request).

ContextCacheCoordinatorStatus finalizeSequenceAdmission(
RequestHandle &request,
int32_t sequenceIndex,
ContextCacheSequenceAdvance const &advance,
std::optional<int32_t> commonStateLength = std::nullopt
)#

The single-slot form of finalizePrefillPublication: record the final chunk’s lookahead token, advance the slot’s committed prefix to the full input, and publish its ready prefix state (the hybrid snapshot captured for this sequence only).

commonStateLength is the prefix a speculative sequence’s draft has materialized (the full input when empty). A Hybrid+spec sequence published its checkpoint during the prefill (publishHybridSpecEndpoint); an EAGLE-flavor sequence’s prompt endpoint publishes at the next decode completion, capped at the common state.

Join one more sequence to a live request: look up its prefix, lease its pages, and bind exactly its page-table row (and its draft page-table row, for a paired speculative lease). Attention deployments without bounded SWA only.

The request may hold no sequence yet: an empty request admitted with no sequences takes its first one here, through the same path as every later one. The sequence inherits the request’s lookup, commit and replay-tail policies unless its admission overrides the lookup policy.

The device KV length for the new slot stays the runtime’s business – its seating sequence owns the cache-manager view – and the ledger is not advanced until advanceSequenceAdmission or finalizeSequenceAdmission reports a committed prefill chunk.

Other sequences may still be in admission, and their chunk work may be in flight: the lease comes from pages no enqueued work references, and the row upload is stream-ordered for a slot no enqueued step reads. Request-wide work (a decode step or a compaction) must be resolved first.

bool retractSequenceAdmission(RequestHandle &request) noexcept#

Undo the most recent admitSequence before its slot ever joined the runtime batch: release the lease, drop the sequence, and clear its page-table row.

Only legal while the admitted sequence is still the batch’s tail and the runtime holds no slot for it – the recovery path for a seating that threw between lease and slot append. A seated slot that failed later stays, terminal from birth, and leaves through the ordinary eviction instead. Retracting from a request that holds no sequence poisons the coordinator: nothing was admitted, so the caller’s bookkeeping is already wrong.

ContextCacheCoordinatorStatus preparePrefill(RequestHandle &request)#

ContextCacheCoordinatorStatus publishHybridSpecEndpoint(
RequestHandle &request,
int32_t slot,
int32_t residentStateLength,
Tensor const &baseHiddenStates,

Publish one Hybrid+spec checkpoint at the stable predecessor boundary. This is the dedicated MTP publication entrypoint; the runtime drives it after the folded draft prefill has materialized the boundary draft state, for a blocking request’s sequence or one in sequence admission. It captures the recurrent state, the paired base+draft partial pages, and the successor-dependent boundary base-hidden row, then commits the exact checkpoint. Skipped for bypass, already-published, or empty prefixes.
